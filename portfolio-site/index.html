🎡 About me

Hello, I'm Smera!

I'm a UX Designer with a background in psychology — which means I don't just design screens, I think about the people behind them: their motivations, behaviours, mental models, and moments of friction.

I've worked across government, startups, and non-profits, bringing the same curiosity and rigour to every project. Whether I'm running discovery interviews or iterating in Figma, I'm always asking why before I ask what.

I believe the best design feels inevitable — like it couldn't have been any other way.

🎢 How I Work

My Design Process

01
🔭
Discover

I start with people — interviews, research, and observation to understand real needs before touching a single wireframe.

02
🧠
Define

Synthesis through affinity mapping, journey maps, and insight frameworks — turning raw data into clear design problems.

03
✏️
Design

Sketches to high-fidelity prototypes, always grounded in the research. I design for clarity first, delight second.

04
🧪
Test & Iterate

Usability tests, feedback loops, and data analysis — design is never done, just getting better with each round.
