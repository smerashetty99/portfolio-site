🎡 About the Designer

Hello, I'm Smera!

I'm a UX Designer who believes great design should feel like magic — seamless, intuitive, and a little bit delightful. I combine psychological understanding with intentional visual craft to create experiences that don't just work, but feel right.

I bring together user research, interaction design, and a deep curiosity about human behaviour to solve meaningful problems. Whether I'm sketching a flow on a napkin or prototyping in Figma, I'm always thinking about the person on the other side of the screen.
