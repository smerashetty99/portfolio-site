GooseConnect: Designing Belonging for Campus Life

Creating a platform that helps University of Waterloo students build authentic, lasting connections beyond the classroom.

Overview

GooseConnect is a conceptual platform crafted for University of Waterloo students to foster genuine connections and combat campus isolation. Frustrated by fragmented channels and the challenge of meeting new friends, our team set out to design a better way for students to discover and connect with peers.

As Team Facilitator and UX Designer, I led research, journey mapping, prototyping, and cross-functional coordination from inception to final design.

App Design UX Research Prototyping Community Journey Mapping

Project Assets

Problem & Solution

The Problem

Many University of Waterloo students feel isolated and hesitant to reach out. Current platforms are too fragmented or overwhelming. Students struggle to find peers open to new friendships, often missing out on events or low-pressure meetups. Loneliness and homesickness persist, especially for newcomers navigating a large campus.

The Solution

GooseConnect offers a safe, intuitive space where students can connect based on shared interests and real-time availability. With interest-based matching, curated events, and conversation starters, GooseConnect lowers the barrier to social interaction and encourages authentic relationships.

Goals

Our motivation stemmed from a desire to address student loneliness by designing a digital product that felt approachable, inclusive, and genuinely helpful. Every design choice was anchored in real student experiences and feedback.

Goal 01

Build a safe, approachable platform that encourages students to connect beyond their classes.

Goal 02

Reduce social barriers and anxiety for students seeking new friendships and activities.

Goal 03

Enable students to discover relevant events and peers in real time, supporting spontaneous and meaningful interactions.

Research & Personas

We developed personas grounded in real student interviews and survey data, focusing on those who felt isolated yet eager to participate more in campus life. Personas shared common traits: newcomers, international students, or introverts struggling to break into existing social circles.

Key pain points uncovered:

  • Anxiety about reaching out to new people in unfamiliar settings
  • Uncertainty about who was open to meeting and forming friendships
  • Not knowing about relevant events in time to attend
  • Feeling overwhelmed by the sheer scale of campus life
  • Homesickness compounded by the difficulty of finding "your people"

Key Insight

"39% of students surveyed say they had experienced loneliness during the previous day." — Inside Higher Ed. This statistic grounded our design decisions throughout the entire process, keeping empathy at the centre.

Customer Journey

The end-to-end journey starts with a student feeling disconnected and searching for ways to belong on campus. After discovering GooseConnect, they experience a simple onboarding that tailors content to their interests.

Key moments in the journey:

  • Browsing a curated feed of events matched to their interests
  • Matching with peers who share similar hobbies and schedules
  • Initiating conversations through built-in icebreakers to reduce awkwardness
  • Receiving timely notifications so they never miss relevant events
  • Adjusting privacy controls to choose their comfort level of visibility

Sketches & Process

Early sketches played a crucial role in exploring how students might navigate GooseConnect. I facilitated group sketching sessions, encouraging divergent thinking on layouts for the connect, events, and chat flows.

These sketches allowed us to visualize different information architectures before committing to wireframes, sparking conversations about what would feel most welcoming and easy for anxious users. Iterating on paper helped us quickly spot and resolve potential confusion points in the journey.

UI Design

The final UI features bold colours and playful elements inspired by the University of Waterloo's unofficial Goose mascot, creating a sense of campus pride and approachability. We opted for a card-based events feed, clean navigation tabs, and soft edges to reduce cognitive load and encourage exploration.

Privacy controls and clear CTAs empower students to choose their comfort level with connections. Every detail — from friendly icebreaker prompts to subtle onboarding animations — reinforces GooseConnect's mission to turn digital interactions into meaningful, real-world friendships.

Learnings & Next Steps

Learnings

  • Thoughtfully designed UX can foster a true sense of belonging, not just usability
  • Facilitating a diverse team taught me the value of inclusive collaboration and clear communication
  • User feedback is essential — testing early and often led to insights we never anticipated during ideation

Next Steps

  • Conduct usability testing with a broader range of students to refine the prototype further
  • Develop a minimum viable product for real-world pilot testing on campus
  • Expand features to support group meetups and integrate university event calendars
