Willie's Chocolates: A Modern Wonka Brand

Reimagining a nostalgic chocolate brand for the digital age — designing a magical, immersive experience for young dreamers and old nostalgics alike.

Overview

Willie's Chocolates is a reimagined, modern-day brand built on the spirit of Willy Wonka — delivering magical, nostalgic chocolate experiences for young dreamers and those who never quite grew up. The project centred on translating that whimsy and wonder into a cohesive digital experience: a brand identity, website, and e-commerce flow that felt as delightful as unwrapping something special.

The challenge was to balance nostalgia with modernity — honouring the playful, fantastical legacy of the source material while creating something that felt fresh, credible, and shoppable in 2025.

Brand Challenge & Vision

The Challenge

Nostalgic brands often struggle online — they feel dated, or they over-correct and lose the magic that made them special. Willie's needed a digital presence that captured childlike wonder without feeling childish, and made the act of buying chocolate feel like an adventure, not just a transaction.

The Vision

A fully immersive digital chocolate factory. Every scroll reveals something new. Product pages feel like unwrapping a gift. The checkout is called "The Golden Ticket." The brand voice is warm, witty, and a little mysterious — like a letter from someone who has been to places you haven't.

Design Strategy

I approached this project with a strong focus on brand coherence — every design decision needed to reinforce the same emotional story. The process moved from brand personality definition through visual language development to interface design.

Brand Personality

Willie's Chocolates is: whimsical but sophisticated, nostalgic but not retro, indulgent but not overwhelming. The visual language draws from deep jewel tones, hand-drawn textures, gold foil accents, and typography that feels like it was set on a vintage letterpress — then given a contemporary, airy layout to breathe in.

Principle 01

Every screen should feel like opening a door to somewhere magical — delight at each interaction.

Principle 02

Nostalgia as warmth, not museum piece — the brand honours the past while feeling confident in the present.

Principle 03

Purchasing should feel like a gift, not a checkout — ritual, anticipation, and reveal built into the flow.

Key Design Decisions

  • Rich, deep colour palette (midnight plum, forest green, burnished gold) contrasted with cream — evoking luxury and warmth simultaneously
  • Illustrated product packaging rendered in 3D to create a sense of tangibility through the screen
  • Micro-animations on hover and scroll that reward curious exploration — confetti on add-to-cart, unwrapping effect on product reveal
  • Narrative-driven product descriptions that tell a story rather than list ingredients
  • "Can't Make It? Order Our Chocolate Online" CTA anchored consistently — acknowledging the magic of in-person while converting digital visitors
  • Streamlined checkout flow themed around "The Golden Ticket" — confirmation email is a ticket reveal animation

Learnings

This project taught me that brand-led UX design is as rigorous as problem-led UX design — just in a different direction. Instead of starting with pain points, you start with a feeling and work backward to the features that create it. The constraint is emotional consistency rather than task efficiency.

Getting the tone right across typography, copy, colour, animation, and interaction took many iterations. The version that worked was always the one where every element told the same story.
